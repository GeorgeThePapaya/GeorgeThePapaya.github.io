<h2> The Whispering Earring (Excerpt) </h2>
<p>In the treasure-vaults of Til Iosophrang rests the Whispering Earring, buried deep
beneath a heap of gold where it can do no further harm.</p>

<p>The earring is a little topaz tetrahedron dangling from a thin gold wire. When worn, it
whispers in the wearer's ear: "Better for you if you take me off." If the wearer ignores
the advice, it never again repeats that particular suggestion.</p>
